About Us

Rooted in the community we serve

Bright Futures Community was founded by local parents and educators who saw a gap: kids needed somewhere safe to go, and to grow, after the school day ended.

Our Mission

To gove every child in our community access to education, mentorship, and a support network - regardless of their family's cicumstances.

Our Vision

A community where a childs's future is shaped by their potential, not their postcode.

Our History

Founded in 2008 by a group of local teachers and parents running a single after-school reading circle out of a church basement, Bright Futures has grown into a full-time nonprofit serving hundreds of families each year, without losing its neighboorhood roots.

Our Values

What guides every program and every decision we make.

Belonging

Every child and family is welcomed exactly as they are.

Accountability

We report openly on outcomes and how funds are used.

Consistency

Kids can count on us being there, week after week.
