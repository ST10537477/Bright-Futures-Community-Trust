Services

Programs built around what families actually need

Every program is free or low-cost, and open to families across the community.

After-School Education

Homework help, small-group tutoring in reading and math, and hands-on enrichment activities — Monday to Friday, 3:00–6:00 PM.

Ages: 6–13  |  Cost: Free

Our certified tutors and trained volunteers work in small groups so every child gets real attention, not just supervision. Snacks and a quiet homework space are provided every session.

Mentors are background-checked, trained, and matched based on shared interests. Most matches meet weekly for a minimum of one school year, giving relationships real time to grow.

Mentorship

One-to-one matching with a trained adult mentor for guidance, encouragement, and a consistent, caring relationship.

Ages: 10–17  |  Cost: Free

Family Support

Food assistance, counselling referrals, and help navigating school, housing, and social services.

Who: Any household with children in our programs  |  Cost: Free

Our Family Support Lead works directly with parents and guardians to connect them with local resources, cutting through paperwork and long waitlists wherever possible.

How to join a program

Getting started takes three simple steps.

1

Submit an enquiry

Tell us which program interests you and a bit about your family.

2

Intake call

Our team calls within 3 business days to confirm fit and availability.

3

Start the program

Your child joins the next available session — most start within two weeks.
