How we help

Three core programs built around what local families told us they needed most.

After-School Education

Homework support, tutoring, and enrichment activities in a safe, welcoming space.

Mentorship

One-to-one mentor matching that helps kids build confidence and long-term goals.

Family Support

Resources, counselling referrals, and food assistance for households in need.
